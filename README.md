# Nobti legal pages

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for the Nobti app, in English, French and
Arabic, published with GitHub Pages at https://nobti-org.github.io/nobti-legal/.

The app links to `<lang>/privacy.html` and `<lang>/terms.html` (`lang` is `en`,
`fr` or `ar`). Keep those paths stable. When the content changes, update the
effective date in all three languages.
