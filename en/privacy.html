Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Nobti is a mobile app for creating, joining and managing waiting queues. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who can see it and the choices you have. It applies to the Nobti app for Android and iOS.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Nobti ("we", "us") is responsible for the personal data processed through the app. You can reach us at nobti.maroc@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account information. Your name, your email address and whether it is verified. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see it or store it in readable form. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Preferences. Your app language, kept on your account so notifications reach you in that language. Your theme choice stays on your device only.
  • Queue activity. For queues you create: the queue name, settings, status and timestamps. For queues you join: your user ID and display name in the queue's member list, and a record of when you join or leave.
  • Notification token. A push notification token for your device, so we can tell you when your turn is coming.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider processes standard technical data, such as IP address, device and app version, to run and secure the service.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report (the error and where it happened in the code, device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier) so we can fix the problem. Crash reports are not linked to your account.
  • Camera. The camera is used only to scan queue QR codes. Images are processed on your device and are never stored or sent to us.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or any payment information.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and verify your email address.
  • To let you create, join, follow and leave queues, and to show queue owners who is waiting.
  • To send notifications about your queues, in your language.
  • To keep the service working, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We use your data only to provide Nobti. We do not sell it, we do not show ads, and we do not use it for profiling or to track you across other apps.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users of a queue. When you join a queue, its owner and other people who open that queue (for example by scanning its QR code) can see your display name and your place in line.
  • Service providers. Nobti runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions, notifications, remote configuration and crash reporting), provided by Google LLC. Google processes the data on our behalf under its data processing terms.
  • Legal requests. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be located outside your country. Google protects it with industry-standard security measures.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data while your account exists. Queues you delete are permanently removed 30 days after deletion. When you delete your account, we delete your account information and remove you from queues, as described in the account deletion instructions.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Moroccan Law No. 09-08 and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its processing, or receive a copy of it. You can edit your name in the app and delete your account at any time. For any other request, email us. You can also contact your data protection authority; in Morocco, that is the CNDP.

8. Children

Nobti is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by authentication and server-side security rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to fix issues quickly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: nobti.maroc@gmail.com.
