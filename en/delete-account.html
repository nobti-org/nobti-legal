Delete your account

Last updated: 8 November 2026

You can delete your Nobti account and its data at any time.

From the app

  1. Open Nobti and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top of the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you can no longer use the app, email nobti.maroc@gmail.com from the address of your Nobti account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Your language preference and notification tokens.
  • Your name and entries in every queue you joined or left.
  • Queues you own: they end immediately for their members and are permanently removed within 30 days.

Nothing about your account is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep it.
